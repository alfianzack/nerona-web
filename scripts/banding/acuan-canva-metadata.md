## Halaman 4

At Canva, metadata is defined as the title
and keywords associated with an element.
They’re important because they directly
affect the searchability of your content.
As creators, you’re responsible for providing
metadata that is safe, inclusive, accurate,
and relevant to the content you submit.

What is metadata?
At Canva, metadata is defined as the title and keywords associated with an element. They’re important because they directly affect the searchability of your content.
As creators, you’re responsible for providing metadata that is safe, inclusive, accurate, and relevant to the content you submit.
